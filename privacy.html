Koi & Cosmos: Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026

Koi & Cosmos is a free wellness web app: stand-up walk timer, focus music, a wake alarm and cosmic themes. It has no accounts, no server and no analytics.

What is stored, and where

Your settings, walk history, tasks, alarm time and any birth details you enter are saved only in this browser's local storage on your device. They are never sent to the developer. Clearing this site's data in your browser deletes them.

Services the app contacts

  • Open-Meteo geocoding: only the city name you type when looking up a birth place is sent, to find its coordinates.
  • Spotify (optional): if you connect Spotify, you sign in on Spotify's own page. The app receives a temporary access token that stays in your browser and is used only to play music. Spotify's privacy policy applies. While the app is in Spotify's development mode, only Spotify accounts the developer has added can connect.
  • Google Fonts: fonts are loaded from Google's servers, which receive your IP address as part of any web request.

Alarms and notifications

The wake alarm only sounds while the app is open. Phones pause web apps when the screen locks or the app is closed, so keep it open and plugged in. The app does not send background notifications.

Not medical advice

Walk reminders, sleep tracking and astrology-based themes are for general wellbeing and fun, not medical advice.
